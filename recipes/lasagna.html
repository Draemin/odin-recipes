Lasagna Recipe

Description

This is a classic lasagna recipe that's perfect for feeding a crowd. It's layered with rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella cheese.

Recipe breakdown:

  • Prep time: 30 minutes
  • Cook time: 2 hours 30 minutes
  • Servings: 12

Ingredients:

  • 12 sheets lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain the excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce and simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  6. Place a layer of lasagna noodles on top of the sauce.
  7. Spread half of the ricotta cheese over the noodles.
  8. Sprinkle half of the mozzarella cheese on top.
  9. Repeat the layers once more.
  10. Top with the remaining mozzarella cheese.
  11. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
