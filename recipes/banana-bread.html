Banana Bread Recipe

Description

This is a simple and delicious banana bread recipe that's perfect for breakfast or a quick snack.

Recipe breakdown:

  • Prep time: 15 minutes
  • Cook time: 1 hour
  • Servings: 8

Ingredients:

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, mix the mashed bananas with the melted butter.
  3. In another bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  4. Add the sugar, egg, and vanilla to the banana mixture and stir well.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.
  6. Pour the batter into a greased 9x5 inch loaf pan.
  7. Bake for 50-60 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
